Infrastructure

The Systems


Genomics England, the NHS Federated Data Platform, and national AI supercomputing. Infrastructure is the invisible therapy.


We track the data pipelines and compute clusters that form the digital scaffolding of the clinic—and the engineering limits that dictate what the science can actually do.

Biology was never silent. It was unread.

For a century we carried its language in fragments — a protein at a time, a pathway at a time, a patient at a time. The instruments were patient. The hands were careful. The ledger filled slowly.

Then the machines learned to read.

Why we exist

FibiOS exists for what comes after that threshold. We are a publication about the moment biology stopped being a body of facts and became a body of text that can be parsed, predicted, and in places rewritten.

We are not here to sell you the future. We are here to read it alongside you — carefully, and in order.

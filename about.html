What we hold to

The Law of Artifacts

A number appears here only if it can be traced to a primary source. Where we cannot verify, we write what is true at the level of confidence the evidence allows.

Primacy of Mechanism

A result without a mechanism is a rumor with a p-value. We explain how a thing works, or we say plainly that it is not yet known.

Restraint

The field does not lack for enthusiasm. It lacks for proportion. We will not sell a decade of biology as a product launch.

The Chora (The Middle Ground)

The Space Between

We acknowledge the vast gap between a model that works in a lab and a therapy that works in a patient. We treat machine learning as a hypothesis generator, not a cure.

Nuance over Noise

A trial result is not a headline. We read the confidence intervals, the p-values, and the limitations. An effect size of 98.4 ml is meaningless without the baseline.

"It is not yet known"

This is a phrase we use often. Acknowledging the boundary of current evidence is not a failure of rigor; it is the definition of it. Where the record is thin, FibiOS says so and stops.

What we refuse

No Prophecy

We do not publish aphorism, decree, or prophecy.

No Medical Advice

Nothing here is a substitute for a clinician who has examined you.

No Pay-for-Play

We do not accept payment to place a claim in an article.

No Spin

We do not report a trial outcome differently because a company would prefer it reported that way.
